## 4. Decision Management

- Record decisions affecting approved scope, requirements, architecture, priorities, milestones, or governance.
- Record the approving authority and approval date.
- Preserve superseded decisions rather than deleting their history.
- Reference decisions from other documents instead of duplicating their full content.
- Apply proportional change control; routine implementation details do not automatically require formal decision entries.
- Do not treat a proposed decision as authorization to implement changes.
