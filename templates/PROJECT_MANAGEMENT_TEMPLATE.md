### Documentation and Change Control

Approved project baselines remain stable unless changes are justified and authorized.

Follow the established workflow:

**Inspect → Discuss → Propose → Approve → Implement → Verify → Commit**

New findings must be classified as blocking defects, existing in-scope requirements, deferred enhancements, or separate workstreams.

A completed phase or milestone must not be reopened without justification and Project Owner approval.

---

**Management Principle:** Maintain one authoritative project status, preserve traceable decisions and evidence, and work toward verifiable completion rather than perpetual review.
