When resuming:

- Check the latest checkpoint and any uncommitted changes.
- Consult authoritative project decisions when necessary.
- Resume from the next authorized action.
- Avoid repeating completed investigations without new evidence.

Significant decisions, milestone completion, and formal closure must also be reflected in their authoritative records.
