# Project Management Framework

A practical, reusable project-management framework for planning, executing, verifying, and closing software development and data projects.

This repository presents my approach to organizing independent technical projects through documented decisions, clear ownership, milestone-based progress, and verifiable completion criteria.

The framework is designed to provide consistent governance without imposing unnecessary processes on projects of different sizes and complexity.

## Purpose

I developed this framework to improve continuity, accountability, and consistency across multiple ongoing projects.

Its objectives are to:

- Define project responsibilities and decision authority.
- Establish clear project scope and measurable milestones.
- Preserve significant decisions and their rationale.
- Distinguish planned work from verified completion.
- Manage changes without losing established requirements.
- Support reliable project handoffs and resumption.
- Maintain reproducible records of project progress.

## Governance and Accountability

I serve as the project owner and decision authority for my independently managed projects.

AI assistants may support planning, documentation, technical investigation, and verification activities. However, recommendations and AI-generated material remain subject to my review and approval.

Project decisions, implementation outcomes, and verification evidence must be accurately represented. AI assistance does not replace human responsibility or independent validation.

## Project Lifecycle

The framework follows four primary stages:

**Define → Execute → Verify → Close**

Milestones are closed only after their acceptance criteria have been evaluated, supporting evidence has been reviewed, and closure has been explicitly approved.

Progress and verification records distinguish completed work from planned, blocked, or unverified activity.

## Standards Maintenance and Versioning

The Master Project Management Standard is maintained through a controlled review and approval process.

Changes to governance requirements are evaluated and recorded in the authoritative private management repository before approved versions are published here.

Individual projects may adopt updated standards at different times. Their adoption status should be supported by project-specific evidence rather than assumed from the publication of a new standard.

## Repository Scope

This repository provides a public presentation of approved project-management resources and selected evidence of their practical application.

It does not contain internal operational handoffs, private project records, confidential information, or the complete history of the authoritative management repository.

Project source code and implementation documentation remain in their respective repositories.

## Development Approach

The framework emphasizes transparent decisions, proportional documentation, incremental improvement, reproducible verification, and responsible use of AI-assisted development.

Its purpose is not to create documentation for its own sake, but to make independently managed technical work easier to understand, maintain, evaluate, and improve.
